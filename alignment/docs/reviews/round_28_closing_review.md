## 2. Limitations and debt disposition

`platform_boarding` is explicitly classified as `scene_class=synthetic_declared` in `SceneConfig`,
the source preflight artifact, and this review. Its observation basis is limited to the trajectory
bounding-box footprint. The seven doors/gates/channels are constructed model elements and are not
presented as observed rolling-stock calibration. DEBT-3 is retired only on this synthetic branch.

DEBT-1 and DEBT-4 are `data_blocked`, with no expiry. DEBT-1 needs observed alighting-emergence
positions and uncertainty to assess the 10 m lateral offset. DEBT-4 needs observed per-lane open,
service-ready, idle-underfed, blocked, opportunity, effective-window, and completion measures to
reconcile nominal service with completed throughput. Neither missing evidence can be generated by
further gate or source-accounting code.

The closing rule is explicit: a debt may be retired only if its named cohort can fail the debt
criterion. Missing external observations are recorded as `data_blocked`, not silently expired.
